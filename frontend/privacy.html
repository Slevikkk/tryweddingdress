Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026 · Effective immediately for all users.

Try Wedding Dress ("we", "us", "the Service") is operated as a personal project providing AI-powered virtual try-on for wedding dresses at tryweddingdress.com. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how we keep it safe. By using the Service you agree to the practices described below.

1. What we collect

1.1 Account data

  • Email address (required for sign-up).
  • Password — stored only as a salted hash by our auth provider; we never see the plaintext.
  • Optional full name and your marketing-email preference.

1.2 Photos you upload

  • Full-body photo of yourself ("input photo") that you submit for a try-on.
  • Optional custom dress photo if you upload one instead of choosing from our catalog.
  • The generated try-on result image.

We treat photos of people as special-category data. They are stored in access-controlled private buckets and are accessible only to you (via your authenticated account) and a minimum number of operators for support and abuse prevention.

1.3 Usage data

  • Try-on history (which dress, when, completed / failed, processing time).
  • Payment records when you purchase credits (we never store full card numbers — they are handled directly by our payment processor).
  • Technical logs: a salted hash of your IP address, user-agent, and timestamps. We use these to fight abuse, not to track individuals.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: log you in, run the AI try-on, show your history, and bill you for credits.
  • To send transactional emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset, payment receipts). These are required and cannot be opted out of while you have an account.
  • To send marketing emails (product updates, partner offers) only if you explicitly opted in. You can change your mind at any time from your account or via the unsubscribe link in every marketing email.
  • To prevent abuse and fraud (e.g. one-account-per-person rules).
  • To improve the AI quality. We do not use your photos to train the model unless you give us separate, explicit consent in writing.

3. Sub-processors and partners

We use the following third-party processors to operate the Service:

  • Supabase (database, auth, storage) — Frankfurt region.
  • FASHN AI (the diffusion model that produces the try-on result). Photos are sent to FASHN as base64 over HTTPS; FASHN's policy applies during processing.
  • Cloudflare (DNS, CDN, email routing).
  • Brevo (transactional email delivery).
  • A payment processor (Stripe or LemonSqueezy) for credit purchases — disclosed at the checkout screen.

These vendors process data only on our instructions and are bound by data-processing agreements. We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep data

  • Account data: while your account is active, plus up to 30 days after you delete it.
  • Input photos and try-on results: kept while your account is active. You can delete any generation from your dashboard, which also removes the underlying images. Deleted items are purged from backups within 30 days.
  • Payment records: 7 years, as required by tax law.
  • Technical logs: 90 days.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA/UK, California, etc.) you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated photos at any time.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Port your data to another service.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails or for any optional processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@tryweddingdress.com. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

7. International transfers

Our database and storage are hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). FASHN AI may process your photos outside the EU. Where required, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers.

8. Security

We use TLS in transit, private buckets with row-level security at rest, and salted hashes for IPs and passwords. No system is perfectly secure; if you suspect a breach affecting your account, contact us immediately.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by email (to addresses on file) and on this page at least 14 days before they take effect.
